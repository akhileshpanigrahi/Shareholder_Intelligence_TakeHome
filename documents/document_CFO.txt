ALERT RULES SUMMARY

All alerts cover filings from June 1 to August 31, 2026. "Event Date" is the transaction date reported by the investor. "Filing Date" is when the SEC received the filing.

ALERT 1: ACTIVIST INTENT (CRITICAL)
Triggers when an investor who last filed a passive Schedule 13G now files an activist Schedule 13D for the same holding.
Why it matters: A 13D filer may seek board seats or push for strategic change. This signals a shift from passive to active ownership.

ALERT 2: OWNERSHIP THRESHOLD CROSSING (WARNING)
Triggers when an investor's ownership moves above or below 5% or 10%.
How we calculate it:
- Investor shares come from their latest filing.
- Total shares outstanding come from the latest 10-Q or 8-K on or before the transaction date.
- Ownership % = investor shares / total shares outstanding. We calculate this ourselves and do not use the investor's reported figure, which may be based on old data.
- We compare against the investor's prior filing. First-time filers start at 0%. No rounding is used.
One filing can trigger both the 5% and 10% alerts.
Why it matters: 5% is the level that requires public disclosure. 10% is a major position with possible control implications. Crossing up suggests a growing stake; crossing down suggests a shrinking one.

ALERT 3: LATE FILING (INFORMATIONAL)
Triggers when a filing is received more than 5 business days after the transaction date.
It appears as a lag (in days) in the watch list, not as a separate alert.
Why it matters: A late filing may point to administrative delays or a complex transaction.
